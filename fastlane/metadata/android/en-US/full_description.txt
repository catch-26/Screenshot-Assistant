Screenshot Assistant replaces the digital assistant app with one job: take a screenshot when you use the assist gesture, then let you crop, draw on and share it.

Setup:

1. Install the app and open it.
2. Pick it under Settings > Apps > Default apps > Digital assistant app, and turn on "Use screenshot" there.
3. Use the assist gesture (for example, long-press the home button or swipe up from a bottom corner).

Features:

- Screenshot through the Android Assist API, no screen recording permission
- Crop, draw with a pen or highlighter, undo and redo
- Share straight to any app, with adjustable image format and quality
- Nothing is saved to storage, so old screenshots don't pile up
- Optional reminder after a restart or app update if Android reset the digital assistant app (needs the notification permission)
- No internet access, no ads, no tracking

Requires Android 14 or newer.
